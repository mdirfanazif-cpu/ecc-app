Step 4 · Compile PDF & file

Finish

Compile into one PDF builds every ticked part (e.g. Cleaning + MK) as one file, laid out like the hardcopy: 0–10 row with the score circled, unit scores, total / max / overall %, definitions and both signature blocks. Filename follows the team's pattern, e.g. APS - 30.9.2026 - Audit 6 - ECC G8-5.pdf.

Then file it: SharePoint → Documents → General → ECC (school and month folder), and send a copy to the ECC and the FME.

Save to SharePoint (your Microsoft 365 account)

Find your ECC folder once per school (e.g. "Irfan - APS"). It is remembered on this device. The compiled PDF is uploaded straight into that folder, so no download is needed.

Step 5 · Send

Email the report

Key the school OM and the MOE SCD once per school. They are kept on this device and come back the next time you pick that school. Nothing is sent from this page: the email is created as a draft in your own Outlook, or copied across, and you press Send there.

Finished audit pages

All five parts of Annex G8/5 are available above. Sample-photo reference pages are in the master: SharePoint → Tender Doc (Original) → Volume 2 – Annexes.

Working tool for the CBRE Zone 9 team. Before making the digital copy the official record in place of wet-ink hardcopy, confirm acceptance with the FM and the school's SCD.

Photos: tap "Add photo" under any element's remark. Scores below 5 need one. Photos print on Appendix pages after the signatures and are referenced as "See P1" in the Remarks column, so the audit pages themselves stay unchanged.
